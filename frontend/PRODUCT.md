# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Technical recruiters and tech leads** deciding whether to hire Nikenver Pulgar as a full-stack developer (Angular/Laravel), evaluating real production work and stack depth.
- **Non-technical business owners** deciding whether to commission a custom system from him (the same relationship he has with Sublimax/Subladmin), who need to trust the outcome without understanding terms like "SSR" or "RBAC."

Copy and case studies must work for both: technical enough to prove depth, plain enough to prove business value.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Nikenver Pulgar, a Full Stack developer (Angular + Laravel) based in Maracaibo, Venezuela, with 10+ years of experience. It exists to win full-time employment and/or freelance/consulting clients by showing real, verifiable production systems instead of generic portfolio filler.

## Positioning

Every case study links to a real, live production system (not a mockup or dead screenshot) — internal Grupo Cobeca platforms, Axsence, Subladmin — including systems that require login credentials the visitor doesn't have, used as proof-of-existence rather than a demo. The site also discloses, in plain language, that Nikenver works as an AI-augmented senior developer (he drives architecture and decisions, AI accelerates implementation, he reviews every line) instead of hiding that workflow. A neighboring portfolio could not truthfully copy either claim.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual ES/EN via a custom Signals-based i18n service (Spanish is the default locale).
- Dark theme is the default visual identity; light theme is an explicit alternative.
- Angular SSR + static prerendering, tuned for both traditional SEO and AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini).
- WhatsApp (`wa.me` link, floating button) is the primary contact channel, alongside email and LinkedIn.
- Deployed on Vercel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend: Angular (Standalone Components, Signals, SSR).
- Content for projects/experience/site-settings lives in local JSON files served through Angular services returning `of(...)` — there is no CMS or backend API for the portfolio's own content yet.
- Several proof links point to real internal production systems (Step Web, SIC, PFL8, etc.) that require credentials the visitor won't have; that is intentional and must not be replaced with static screenshots.
- Backend/case-study projects themselves (Subladmin, Axsence, Cobeca systems) are built with Laravel, PostgreSQL/SQL Server, and other stacks documented per project.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim needs a real, checkable artifact behind it (a live link, a production login, a repo) — no placeholder projects or invented metrics.
2. Spanish-first, bilingual by default — the primary market is Venezuela/LatAm; English is the secondary audience, not the assumed one.
3. Disclose the AI-augmented workflow openly rather than hiding it; judgment and review stay human, AI speeds up execution.
4. Write for both audiences in the same copy — technical depth paired with plain-language business outcomes, not jargon walls that only work for one reader.
5. Personal, human details (family, hobbies, side expertise like fragrance consulting) are deliberate identity choices, not filler — they humanize the person behind the stack and should be kept accurate to how the user actually describes himself (see regionally natural phrasing, not generic synonyms).
